Welcome, Dads

If you've ever had the joy of seeing a positive pregnancy test with your wife, you've probably felt overwhelmed with joy. You probably continued to feel overwhelmed once you heard your baby's heartbeat for the very first time, and again overjoyed when you first say them moving their tiny arms and legs. Perhaps you felt elated when you saw them at your 20-week anatomy scan, seeing their individual fingers as your baby danced in your wife's tummy.

And, since you're here, you've experienced the utter heartbreak when you found out your baby no longer had a beating heart. Maybe it was in the first 10 weeks, or maybe before the end of the first trimester. It might've been at your 20-week appointment, or even at thirty weeks. And, for some of us, maybe it was in the final weeks as you anxiously awaited the birth of your baby, only to be told the worst nws of all.
