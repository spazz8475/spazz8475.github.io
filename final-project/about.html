About Us

Hi there, my name is Justin and I'm a father who's lost three babies to miscarriage and stillbirth. All of these losses happened in the first few years of my marriage to my wonderful wife and we've been wrecked by what's happened. If you're reading this, you're probably in the same boat as us. I'm sorry for what you've been through; it's never easy to talk about losing a baby, especially as a man.

I've wanted a space to talk about my babies and that's one of the inspirations behind this website. Keeping their memory alive here allows me to always find a space where they can live on, even if it's on a small interent page.
